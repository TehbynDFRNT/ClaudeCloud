## Q1. Price map: comparable 20" dual-stage whole-house systems, and where a stainless-cabinet dual-stage unit sits

### Takeaway
Supply-only prices fall into three tiers:
- **Bare bracket-mounted twin 20" big-blue systems:** about A$170–800 (eBay about A$170; specialist online stores A$499–799).
- **Covered or enclosed dual-stage systems:** about A$975–1,740. AquaSafe's twin 20" with a stainless stand and cover is A$975. Puretec's WH2-55 dual is A$1,183, and the WH2-55AMC with an aluminium cover is A$1,739.
- **Premium enclosed 3-stage systems:** about A$2,500–5,900. Pure Water Systems' Colorbond triple is A$2,895; Puretec FilterWall ranges from A$3,145 to A$5,899.

Installed, a 2-stage system is quoted at A$1,300–1,800 by a plumber cost guide. A Sydney installer selling through an in-home pitch charges "about $3000" plus A$400 a year. A stainless-cabinet dual-stage unit like the PWF-203BC belongs at the top of the covered-dual tier: about A$975–1,200 supply-only retail, A$2,000–3,000 installed. The A$2,999 installed price can be supported, but only with a consult-led sale. It sits above the plumber-quoted band for 2-stage systems.

### Inferences
- **Positioning.** A twin 20" stainless-cabinet unit competes most directly with AquaSafe's A$975 SS stand-and-cover twin and Puretec's A$1,739 WH2-55AMC. A realistic supply-only shelf price for an unknown brand is **A$899–1,199 inc GST**. Above that, Puretec's brand and 10-year warranty win.
- **Retail premiums.** Enclosures add roughly A$400 at retail (PWS Colorbond) to about A$550 (Puretec WH2-55AMC vs WH2-55). Stainless versus bare costs about A$375 at AquaSafe (A$975 vs A$599).
- **Installed.** Plumber-quoted 2-stage jobs cluster at A$1,300–1,800. The A$2,999 price point is a consult-led, sales-driven price; the Sydney data point and WAA's finance-led pitch fit this. A plain plumber channel does not support it.

## Q3. Landed cost on 64 units (ex GST)

### Takeaway
At US$290 FOB, the 64 units land at about **A$486 per sellable unit ex GST** (range A$481–496 depending on carton size). That rises to **A$507 if no ChAFTA Certificate of Origin is held**.
- **Cash:** about **A$30.2k ex GST**, plus about A$3.0k of GST to fund until the BAS refund, for a peak of about A$33.2k.
- **Where the money goes:** the FOB is 88% of landed cost, and freight is only A$34–48 a unit. Negotiating the FOB is the only lever that matters.
- **The quote is high:** comparable Chinese stainless-covered big-blue systems are listed at US$100–350.

## Q4. Channel economics: plumber wholesale, direct supply-and-install, and online

### Takeaway
- **(a) Wholesale at A$1,100 ex GST is not realistic.** It is about 24% above the *consumer* price of a comparable stainless-covered twin (AquaSafe A$975 inc, A$886 ex). It is also roughly equal to the RRP of Puretec's branded 55 L/min dual (WH2-55 A$1,076 ex; cartridge length not confirmed) and about 70% of the RRP of Puretec's covered dual (WH2-55AMC A$1,581 ex), both with 10-year warranties. A defensible trade price is **A$600–800 ex**, which gives **A$52–248 gross profit a unit (9–31%)**, or A$3–16k on 64 units.
- **(b) Direct supply-and-install at A$2,999 inc is the only channel with real margin.** It makes about **A$1,036 a job when the owner sells (38% of net revenue), A$763 with a 10%-commission salesperson, and A$322 in a high-cost case**, or A$21–66k on 64 jobs. It needs lead generation, a consult-led sale and a licensed plumber.
- **(c) The online supply-only channel exists**, but at AquaSafe-parity A$975 it makes about A$156 a unit on an own site (18%), or about A$67 on a marketplace. A$800 is break-even.

### Cited Findings
- **Competitor trade and retail anchors:** see Q1. Puretec RRPs are "suggested retail price. Reseller price may vary". — [Puretec WH2-55](https://www.puretec.com.au/WH2-55). Tradelink shows A$893 publicly, with trade prices behind a login. — [Tradelink](https://tradelink.com.au/high-flow-whole-house-dual-water-filter-system-10in-30lpm-lead-free-319756/)

## Q5. Cartridge annuity

### Takeaway
