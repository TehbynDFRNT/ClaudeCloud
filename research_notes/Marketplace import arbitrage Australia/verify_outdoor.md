*Verification run 9 Oct 2026. All prices AUD incl. GST and observed 9 Oct 2026 unless another date is given. "Since <date>" = the date the current price took effect in the retailer's price-history table on PriceHipster.*

**Shared base assumptions used for every number below (model inputs, not findings):** US$1 = A$1.4376 (AUD/USD 0.6956, RBA 8 Oct 2026). Freight door-delivered: 40HQ A$148/CBM × 68 usable CBM (A$10,064 per box); 20GP A$211/CBM × 28 CBM (A$5,908 per box); LCL A$244 per W/M (greater of CBM or tonnes) + A$900 per shipment. Duty 0% with ChAFTA CoO (5% sensitivity on FOB). GST-registered (import GST reclaimed); net revenue = price ÷ 1.1; no Marketplace fee (pickup). Inspection A$400 per factory per order. Defect/damage 3% of landed cost. Handling/storage A$11/CBM/month × 3 = A$33/CBM. Compliance/labels as found (estimates labelled).

**Formula (model):** L0 = FOB(A$) + freight/unit + duty + inspection/unit + compliance/unit (+ China consolidation/unit in the mixed box). Cost/unit = L0 × 1.03 + A$33 × CBM/unit. GP/unit = price/1.1 − cost. Break-even price = cost × 1.1. "Cash" = units × (FOB + freight) + inspection + compliance, ex-GST; import-GST float ≈ 10% of (FOB + freight), reclaimed later. LCL is cheaper than a 20GP below 20.5 CBM ((5,908 − 900) ÷ 244), i.e. below ~263 ovens, ~335 hammocks or ~144 Fanpin egg chairs.

**Compliance inferences.**
- Sell the oven with **no gas burner, no gas inlet, and no "gas-ready/multi-fuel with burner" claims**. An "optional" burner, even sold separately or as a link to a Chinese burner kit, would make the seller a supplier of an uncertified Type A appliance (the ESV and Ooni pages tie certification to a specific oven-plus-burner model). That is an inference from the cited pages; no regulator text on accessories was found.
- Specify a **steel or silicone handle in the PO** instead of Otia's bamboo handle, or obtain a treatment certificate and expect possible inspection holds. Bamboo parts pull the consignment into the BICON bamboo case (inference from cited DAFF wording).
- Total-fire-ban days prohibit outdoor use of wood/pellet ovens in NSW (and similar bans exist in other states; not verified here). This blunts the "summer gift" thesis and should be disclosed to buyers.
- No Australian mandatory standard for solid-fuel cooking ovens was found in this pass (gap, not a finding of absence).

**Verdict: REVISED (down). Score 4/10 (was 6).** The factory inputs verify (Otia US$43.50–44.50, 0.078 CBM, 11.3 kg), but the product is an 11" oven with a bamboo handle, and the price to beat is ~A$123–136 (Costway on Amazon AU and direct), not A$135.95–149.95. At a price that actually undercuts Costway (A$110–125), a first LCL earns A$0–14/unit (0–12%) on ~A$19k cash; only a full 20GP (A$24/unit at A$125) or 40HQ (A$32/unit) is worth doing, and 359–873 ovens is a lot for pickup sales of an 11 kg, parcel-friendly item where pickup gives no delivery edge.
