*Status: IN PROGRESS (checkpoint 1). Verification run 9 Oct 2026. All prices AUD incl. GST unless stated; all observed 9 Oct 2026 unless another date is given.*

**Method and access notes (9 Oct 2026)**
- PriceHipster search (`https://pricehipster.com/?q=...`) and product pages were fetched directly. Product pages carry a dated "Price History" table, so each floor below has the date the current price took effect.
- Gardeon's public Shopify catalogue (`gardeon.com.au/products.json`, 4 pages, about 1,000 products) gave Gardeon's own-site list prices and shipping weights.
- Amazon AU: Best Sellers pages were fetched directly. Product (`/dp/`) pages loaded, but the session's delivery location is "United States", so they show "No featured offers available" and no buy-box price. The all-offers panel says "no other sellers matching your location". Changing the postcode hit a bot challenge (HTTP 202) and was not retried, in line with the brief and with the parallel benchmark notes, which hit the same wall. Amazon prices therefore come only from Best Sellers "offers from" prices or are labelled "search summary".
- Kmart, Bunnings and Big W pages were not fetched directly (403 in earlier runs). Kmart figures are WebSearch summaries, labelled.
