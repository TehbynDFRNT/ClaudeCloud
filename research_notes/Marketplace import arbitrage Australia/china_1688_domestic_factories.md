# China domestic factory-direct supply (1688 / Chinese-language sources) for AU Facebook Marketplace products (AUD 100–400)

Research date: 2026-10-09. Scope: Chinese-language and domestic-platform angle (1688, Chinese registries and industry sources), plus a few export-platform price points used only for the domestic-vs-export comparison. All CNY prices below are as displayed on the source on or before 2026-10-09. Unless stated, the source does not say whether the CNY price includes VAT (含税) or what the MOQ is.

## Q1. Production clusters (产业带) and named source factories with domestic CNY prices, by product

### Takeaway
The likely clusters for most products are confirmed by 1688 supplier data and Chinese-language sources. They are Yongkang, Wuyi and Jinhua for walking pads, Ningjin/Dezhou plus Nantong and Dingzhou for strength equipment, Cixi/Ningbo for compressor car fridges, Shenzhen/Guangzhou/Zhongshan for power stations, Foshan-Shunde plus Jiangmen/Enping for PE rattan, Anji (with Bazhou at the budget end) for office chairs, Changzhou and Ningbo for standing desks, Ganzhou-Nankang and Foshan for gas-lift beds, Pingxiang/Xingtai for ride-ons and Wenling for cat trees. Domestic 1688 "card" prices are often far below export FOB. However, they are usually the lowest-priced SKU, the product spec behind them is not visible, and they carry no MOQ, VAT or export terms. They are a starting point for an RFQ, not a landed-cost basis. Diesel heaters, large dog crates, backyard trampolines, pop-up gazebos and swags have weak or no 1688 domestic price evidence in this research.

#### Access and method notes (how the data below was obtained)
- 1688 search, offer and SEO pages (s.1688.com, detail.1688.com, www.1688.com/jiage|chanpin) returned an Alibaba anti-bot "punish … action=deny" page to automated fetches. They were not bypassed and no login was attempted. Alibaba.com search was also "punish"-blocked. Qichacha returned a JavaScript anti-bot challenge, Tianyancha returned HTTP 419 and Aiqicha returned an empty page. Baidu web search returned a wappass captcha after about 5 queries and Sogou returned an antispider page. All of these were left alone. — [1688 search (blocked)](https://s.1688.com/selloffer/offer_search.htm?keywords=可调节哑铃&charset=utf8); [Qichacha (challenge)](https://www.qcc.com/web/search?key=浙江大跑科技有限公司); [Tianyancha (419)](https://www.tianyancha.com/search?key=浙江大跑科技有限公司)
- wiki.1688.com ("1688买家百科/采购百科") is 1688's own public content site and was readable. Each article embeds live 1688 supplier cards showing the company name, 超级工厂/实力商家 badge, years on 1688, 回头率, 好评率, a displayed CNY unit price (lowest tier, MOQ not shown), and the shop and offer URL. The article's main product is identified in its JSON-LD "mainEntityOfPage" offer URL. The surrounding prose is AI-written marketing that carries the footer "本内容均源自互联网，内容仅供参考" and sometimes contradicts itself, so prose claims are labelled as such. — [Example page](https://wiki.1688.com/zh/fitness/WKg04vsn5aicxs); [footer example](https://wiki.1688.com/zh/WKfnufxzo1c740)
- Data marked "(snippet)" comes from a search-engine snippet of a 1688 or company page that could not be opened. It is unverified.
- Export comparators come from Made-in-China.com search pages, which are server-rendered and showed FOB price and MOQ. They were fetched on 2026-10-09.

## Q2. 1688 supplier signals (实力商家, 超级工厂, 深度验厂/实地认证, 源头工厂, years, 回头率): what they mean and how reliable they are

### Takeaway
1688's factory signals are partly platform-verified. 源头工厂 requires a production-type licence cross-checked against the national registry. 超级工厂 and 实地认证 involve on-site or third-party checks. However, the badges are bought into or applied for, they appear on 1–2-year-old shops, and they sit side by side with trading shops (商行/贸易/商贸) in the same results. Use them as a filter, then confirm with the registry and with photos or video of the actual line.

### Cited Findings
- 1688 describes its "源头工厂" vetting as follows. — [wiki.1688 源头工厂认证全流程拆解 (2026-05-07)](https://wiki.1688.com/zh/WKfl9os8222xhc)
  - The business licence must be production-type, or its scope must include "制造"/"加工". The system checks this in real time against 国家企业信用信息公示系统.
  - The 工厂名片 (factory profile) must be at least 80% complete and the shop rated 4 stars or more.

  - The 超级工厂 badge.

## Q3. Registry verification (Qichacha / Tianyancha / Aiqicha / official GSXT): what to check, and APIs and costs

### Takeaway
Use the free official 国家企业信用信息公示系统 (GSXT) plus Qichacha, Tianyancha or Aiqicha to confirm the following, then compare the registered and production addresses:
- 经营范围 includes 生产/制造/加工, not only 销售/批发
- Registered and paid-in capital
- 参保人数 from annual reports
- Establishment date
- Related companies (a factory often pairs with a separate 进出口/贸易 company)
- Litigation, 失信 and administrative penalties

Qichacha runs a paid per-call Open API. Its prices are not publicly listed and require contacting sales. All three commercial registries blocked automated access during this research.

### Cited Findings
- 1688 itself cross-checks licence type and 经营范围 ("制造""加工") against 国家企业信用信息公示系统. It also tells buyers to verify that the licence address matches the factory on that system. — [wiki.1688](https://wiki.1688.com/zh/WKfl9os8222xhc); [wiki.1688](https://wiki.1688.com/zh/WKfizlowuxxnuo)
- 1688's on-site certification (实地认证) reviews 3 months of utility bills, social-insurance payment records (社保缴纳记录) and purchase invoices "佐证生产活跃度", i.e. as evidence that production is active. — [wiki.1688](https://wiki.1688.com/zh/WKfl9os8222xhc)
- Qichacha Open API (openapi.qcc.com):
  - ApiCode 410 企业工商信息 returns 企业类型, 注册资本, 经营范围 and 营业期限.
  - ApiCode 735 企业工商详情 returns shareholders, key personnel and change records.
  - ApiCode 887 裁判文书核查 covers court judgements, and 740/741/742 cover 失信, 被执行人 and 限制高消费.
  - The page shows no per-call price. It has a "余额充值" (prepaid balance) link and gives 400-088-8275 and api@qcc.com for pricing.
  - 行政处罚 and 参保人数 endpoints were not listed on the landing page.
  - Source: [Qichacha Open API](https://openapi.qcc.com/)
- A third-party SDK confirms ApiCode 410 = 企业工商照面 and that calls are billed per call (search summary). — [go-qcc-sdk](https://beta.pkg.go.dev/github.com/chaunsin/go-qcc-sdk)
- Tianyancha's open-platform URL redirected to its data-services page, and no pricing was retrieved. — [Tianyancha data](https://www.tianyancha.com/data)
- Qichacha web search returned an anti-bot JavaScript challenge, Tianyancha returned HTTP 419 and Aiqicha returned an empty page. None could be used to check sample factories. — [Qichacha](https://www.qcc.com/web/search?key=浙江大跑科技有限公司); [Aiqicha](https://aiqicha.baidu.com/s?q=浙江大跑科技有限公司)
- Example of registry-type facts surfaced through 1688 company pages:
  - 山东博朗特健身器材 was founded 2019-12-23 with a stated factory address in the Ningjin development zone. — [1688 factory page (snippet)](https://www.1688.com/factory/b2b-22095039396927343d.html)
  - 浙江骑炫电子科技 was founded 2017-06-06. — [aichangjia](https://www.aichangjia.com/factory/203047.html)
  - 邢台牛晨科技 was founded June 2024, according to the 1688 article. — [wiki.1688](https://wiki.1688.com/zh/kids-ride-on-cars/WKfr0krr4uxdds)

### Inferences
- A practical check sequence for each shortlisted supplier:
  1. Search the exact 公司名 on GSXT, Qichacha or Aiqicha.
  2. Confirm status 存续 and an establishment date of 3 or more years ago. Note that several 超级工厂 badges sit on shops with only 1–2 years on 1688; for example, 邢台牛晨科技 was founded June 2024.
  3. Confirm 经营范围 contains "制造/生产" for the product.
  4. Check 参保人数 in the latest annual report. Fewer than about 10 insured staff is inconsistent with a claimed multi-thousand-m² plant.
  5. Check whether the registered address is in the cluster town (e.g. 慈溪附海/龙山, 宁津开发区, 永康/武义, 平乡/广宗, 顺德乐从/龙江).
  6. Look for a sister 进出口 company (common for export billing).
  7. Review litigation, 失信 and 行政处罚, especially product-quality (质监/市场监管) penalties.
- A cheaper bulk option is paid Qichacha API calls for ApiCode 410/735/887 on a shortlist of 20–50 suppliers. Budget needs a quote. No public price was obtainable.

### Gaps
- Per-call prices for Qichacha, Tianyancha and Aiqicha APIs, and whether Aiqicha offers an API at all, were not found.
- Whether 参保人数 is returned by Qichacha's standard APIs was not confirmed.

## Q5. Buying route for an Australian buyer: agents, consolidation, payment, export declaration, ChAFTA CoO, AU certifications

### Takeaway
The workable route is:
1. Shortlist on 1688.
2. Pay through a sourcing agent or a cross-border payment rail (WorldFirst / "1688 World Pay"; Wise/Payoneer to RMB accounts).
3. Have goods delivered domestically to an agent or forwarder warehouse in China.
4. Have the agent or forwarder act as exporter of record (customs declaration and documents) and consolidate LCL or FCL to Australia.

Most 1688 micro-factories ship only within China and will not handle export documents. Export-registered factories (with export licences, BSCI and Australian customers) can. AU certifications (RCM/EESS, AS/NZS) usually exist only at export-oriented factories.

### Cited Findings
- "Most suppliers on 1688 only ship within China." Buyers need a China-based forwarder that can consolidate multiple suppliers before international air, sea or rail freight. Foreigners typically pay RMB via Payoneer or Wise, and PayPal and cards are usually not supported. 1688's cross-border channel was described as "only available to registered Chinese entities", and agents such as Superbuy and Wegobuy handle transactions. — [BigSeller (2025-06-26)](https://www.bigseller.pro/blog/articleDetails/3447/1688-sourcing-guide.htm)
- WorldFirst lets buyers pay a supplier's Chinese bank account after verifying it against business registration, with "zero transfer fees" between WorldFirst accounts. Its Pingxiang guide advises "Pay through 1688 World Pay for buyer protection" and says verified suppliers have a business licence and export licence. This conflicts with BigSeller's earlier statement and suggests 1688 overseas payment options have expanded. — [WorldFirst Pingxiang](https://sourcing.worldfirst.com/cities/pingxiang)
- Agent fees and services (Supplyia):
  - 5–9% of order value for sourcing, or from 5% for purchasing from existing suppliers
  - China consolidation from US$10, including "unified exporter of record" and "export documentation"
  - Shipping modes: air, sea, express, rail, DDP and LCL/FCL; inspection visits about US$180–220
  - The site does not mention Australia.
  - Sources: [Supplyia pricing](https://www.supplyia.com/our-services/pricing/); [Supplyia home](https://www.supplyia.com/)
- Export-capable factories show these markers: Huamao (Wenling cat trees) lists an export licence no. and FOB/EXW/CIF terms, ships via Ningbo/Shanghai and names Australia as a main market. Tingwei (Cixi fridges) has BSCI, ISO/TS 16949 and a Halfords ODM project. — [Huamao](https://tzhuamao.en.made-in-china.com/); [Tingwei](https://cn.tingweicn.com/about/)
- WorldFirst notes that "Not all factories can certify for export markets" and tells buyers to look for AS/NZS 2088 (prams/strollers) for Australia. — [WorldFirst Pingxiang](https://sourcing.worldfirst.com/cities/pingxiang)
- 1688 promotes contract orders (合约订单) with deposit on order and balance on receipt. It also promotes 先采后付 (pay later) on some offers, such as the ¥243 Nantong squat rack. — [wiki.1688](https://wiki.1688.com/zh/WKfnufxzo1c740); [wiki.1688](https://wiki.1688.com/zh/WKg09onmsgh7gg)
- Freight-forwarder sourcing portals mirror 1688 listings in USD. One example is Ship4wd's sourcing site (search result). — [Ship4wd sourcing (search result)](https://sourcing-sb.ship4wd.com/product/mens-and-womens-dipped-small-dumbbell-home-exercise-yoga-jumping-dumbbell-hexagon-thin-arm-dumbbell-spot-support)
- Official ChAFTA/rules-of-origin pages (DFAT, ABF, MOFCOM FTA portal) returned 503 or 403 during this research. — [DFAT ChAFTA](https://www.dfat.gov.au/trade/agreements/in-force/chafta); [ABF FTAs](https://www.abf.gov.au/importing-exporting-and-manufacturing/free-trade-agreements); [MOFCOM FTA](http://fta.mofcom.gov.cn/Australia/australia_special.shtml)

### Inferences
- From background knowledge, not verified this session: a ChAFTA Certificate of Origin is issued in China by China Customs (GACC) or CCPIT to the exporter, who must be registered with them. Australia also accepts RCEP origin documents. A domestic-only 1688 factory without customs registration cannot easily issue one itself. The practical fix is to have the agent, forwarder or a 外贸综合服务企业 (e.g. Alibaba 一达通) act as exporter of record and apply for the CoO. Alternatively, use a factory that already exports. The researchers covering landed costs should confirm the ABF low-value threshold below which no proof of origin is needed.
- "买单出口" (buying another firm's export declaration) is a grey practice. It can break the link between the CoO, the commercial invoice and the actual manufacturer. Prefer agents who declare in their own name with correct manufacturer details.
- AU electrical compliance: an RCM and EESS-registered Australian Responsible Supplier is required for in-scope mains equipment. Walking pads and power stations with AC chargers or outlets, and 220/240V variants of diesel heaters, are the most exposed. 1688 domestic SKUs are built for Chinese CCC/GB plugs and standards. Expect to need the export variant (SAA plug, AS/NZS test reports) from export-oriented factories rather than 1688 micro-factories.
- For bulky items (sofas, beds, racks, trampolines), consolidating several 1688 suppliers into one LCL or 20' FCL via a Ningbo/Shenzhen forwarder is the main cost lever. Buying from one cluster (e.g. Foshan furniture, or Yongkang fitness) simplifies pickup.

### Gaps
- ChAFTA/RCEP CoO issuance rules, the AU low-value threshold and the AU duty status per product were not verified (official sites unavailable).
- Fees for Australia-focused 1688 agents and Australia-specific consolidators (A$/CBM to Sydney, Melbourne or Brisbane) were not obtained.
- Whether 1688's "World Pay" or other overseas buyer programmes are open to Australian buyers in 2026 was not confirmed.

### Inferences
- The presence of SAA cords and AS/NZS 3112 plugs on 1688 means a domestic factory could in principle fit an AU plug set. Fitting a plug does not deliver RCM/EESS compliance for the finished appliance.
