## Method and access notes (read first)
- 1688.com search, offer and SEO pages (s.1688.com, detail.1688.com, www.1688.com/jiage|chanpin) returned an Alibaba anti-bot "punish ... action=deny" page to automated fetches. Not bypassed. Login was not attempted.
- wiki.1688.com ("1688采购百科 / 1688买家百科") is 1688's own public content site and is readable. Each article embeds live supplier cards with: company name, 1688 badge (超级工厂/实力商家), years on 1688, 回头率 (repeat-purchase rate), 好评率, displayed CNY price (lowest tier, MOQ not shown on card), shop URL and offer URL. These cards are the main source of 1688 price and supplier data below. The article prose is AI-generated marketing and is treated as low-reliability.
- Some company and price data comes from search-engine snippets of 1688 pages that could not be opened. These are labelled "(snippet)".
