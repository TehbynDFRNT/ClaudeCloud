# AU retail price benchmarks ("price to beat") for Marketplace import resale, AUD $100–$400

Observation date for all prices: 2026-10-09 unless stated. DRAFT, being written in stages.

Method notes:
- Kmart, Kogan, eBay AU, Bunnings, OzBargain and Buywisely returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch. Their prices below come from search-engine index snippets of the product pages, labelled "(snippet)". They were not live-verified, so the listing may have changed.
- Facebook Marketplace search needs a login, so it was not accessed: https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/sydney/search/?query=adjustable%20dumbbells
- Amazon.com.au search pages loaded via curl, but Amazon showed "Deliver to United States" for this server's IP. That view hides most AU-warehoused bulky items, such as Everfit or Lifespan products. Those results are not used as AU floor prices. Setting an AU postcode hit a bot-check interstitial, which was not bypassed.
