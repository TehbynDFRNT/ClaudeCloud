### Cited Findings
- ARB FY26 (year to 30 June 2026): sales A$702.0m (down 3.8%), NPAT A$92.4m (down 5.2%), underlying PBT A$118.6m (down 10.5%); 2H26 PBT rose 1.9% as AUD normalised vs Thai baht — [StockWireX, Aug 2026](https://stockwirex.com/asx-stock-news/consumer-discretionary/arb-corporation-fy26-results-august-2026/) (search-result summary; secondary source). One secondary source reports FY26 gross margin 57.6% vs 56.7%; a data aggregator (Eulerpool) shows 32.7% on a broader cost definition — treat the ~57% "materials-only" GM as the comparable figure; 1H26 materials & consumables were 43.7% of sales vs 41.4% prior half (i.e., materials-only GM ~56.3% vs 58.6%) — [Eulerpool](https://eulerpool.com/stock/ARB-Stock-AU000000ARB5/grossmargin); [Montgomery 1H26](https://www.montinvest.com/investor-insights/insights/arbs-half-year-2026-results) (via search summary; primary ASX release not yet fetched).
- 2H26 PBT A$60.7m vs 1H26 A$58.0m and 2H25 A$63.0m; ARB pushed two list-price rises (~2% from Oct 2025; 3.5-4.0% from Apr 2026) and management attributed 2H margin recovery to those price rises plus easier AUD/THB; AUD/THB hedged ~23 to Nov 2026; R&D ~A$20m/yr (c.A$4m capitalised) with a planned 10-15% uplift in engineering spend; fitter retention improving — [Roger Montgomery, "ARB's FY26 - a look under the hood", 31 Aug 2026](https://rogermontgomery.com/arbs-fy26-a-look-under-the-hood/).
- ARB manufactures in Thailand (Rayong, Eastern Seaboard Industrial Estate: plants "ORA 1" and "ORA 2", plus a planned 20,000 sqm facility from 2019) as well as Australia — [ARB corporate presentation (AustCham Thailand, 2020)](https://www.austchamthailand.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/ARB_Corporate_Presentation.pdf). 4X4 Australia reports ARB builds MT64/BP-51 shocks and the Earth Camper in its Thai factories — [4X4 Australia, "50 years of ARB"](https://www.4x4australia.com.au/gear/50-years-of-arb). An unverified forum claim says ARB's bars for older models come from Thailand and current-model bars are made in Australia — [AULRO forum](https://www.aulro.com/afvb/l319-discovery-3-and-4-a/251551-d3-bull-bar-pricing-2-print.html) (anecdotal).
- US customs data (ImportGenius) for ARB's US importer Air Locker Inc: top supplier ARB Corporation Limited (Australia, 1,825 containers, latest 2026-09-19); ARB Thailand Limited ("automotive part and accessories", latest 2026-10-01); BAS Intertrade LP (Thailand/Vietnam origin); Belton Springs Sdn Bhd (Malaysia, leaf springs); Hangzhou Chengquan Import & Export (China, "roof top tent", 2026-09-15); Off Road Accessories Ltd (Hong Kong, 365 containers to 2024); IPF Trading (Japan, lights); Mobicool International (China, fridges, to 2018) — [ImportGenius: Air Locker Inc](https://www.importgenius.com/importers/air-locker-inc) (fetched 2026-10-10). i.e., ARB's own factories make the steel/suspension core; outsourced items include leaf springs (Malaysia), rooftop tents (China), lights (Japan).
- Rhino-Rack: Clarus Corp bought Rhino-Rack (founded Sydney 1992) in 2021 for ~US$198m (A$255m), on TTM net sales ~US$90m (A$115m) and adj. EBITDA ~US$16.7m (A$21.5m) — EBITDA margin ~18.6% (my calc), price ~11.9x EBITDA; ~80% of sales ANZ, 20% North America; channels: distributors, retail, OEM, e-commerce, growing DTC — [Clarus press release, SEC EX-99.1, 1 Jun 2021](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/913277/000110465921077020/tm2118432d1_ex99-1.htm).
- Rhino-Rack USA's US-import suppliers (ImportGenius): Winbo-Dongjian Automotive (China, 189 containers, "auto accessories (ladder & kit)", latest 2026-09-15); King Rack Industrial (Taiwan, 45 containers); Ningbo Lixin Tour Products (China); Shenzhen Runpeng Precision Hardware ("roof-rack", 2026-09-03); Ningbo Weibo Outdoor (awning parts); Ningbo hitch-rack makers; plus Rhino-Rack Australia/Rhino-Rack LLC (Australia origin, "roof racks & accessories", 2026-09-22) — [ImportGenius: Rhino-rack USA](https://www.importgenius.com/importers/rhino-rack-usa) (fetched 2026-10-10). So a large share of the "Australian" premium rack brand's range is Chinese/Taiwanese-made.
- REDARC's US entity (Redarc Corporation, Grapevine TX) shows 33 shipments 2006-2025 with top partner Cotek Electronic (Taiwan power electronics) — [ImportGenius: Redarc Corporation](https://www.importgenius.com/importers/redarc-corporation) (search summary). Dometic USA's most recent supplier: Dometic (Zhuhai) Technology, China — [ImportGenius: Dometic USA](https://www.importgenius.com/importers/dometic-usa) (search summary).
- TJM: bought in 2015 for A$22.2m by Aeroklas (subsidiary of Thai-listed Eastern Polymer Group) from CMI Ltd — [GoAuto, 24 Feb 2015](https://goauto.com.au/news/general-news/parts/high-profile-4wd-supplier-tjm-sold-to-aeroklas/2015-02-24/23272.html); >80 outlets by its 50th year (2023) — [4X4 Australia](https://www.4x4australia.com.au/news/tjm-celebrates-50th-anniversary).
- Ironman 4x4 says its bull bars are engineered in-house at its Melbourne HQ "to ADR standards"; no source found confirming where they are fabricated — [search summary of ironman4x4.com.au](https://www.ironman4x4.com.au/collections/bull-bars).
- 4WD Supacentre ("Kings"/Adventure Kings) describes itself as a discounter selling its own-brand gear direct, >31 stores, ">750,000 customers", range >300 products, trading in every state except NT (company self-descriptions) — [BHG promo page](https://www.bhg.com.au/promo-codes/4wd-supacentre/); [Real Estate Source](https://www.realestatesource.com.au/vella-family-leases-showroom-on-ex-aldi-site/); [Bundaberg council news, Nov 2024](https://www.brcnow.bundaberg.qld.gov.au/2024/11/29/4wd-supacentre-opens-in-bundaberg/).
