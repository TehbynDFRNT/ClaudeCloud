# ASX/NZX brand, distributor and retail layer margins (FY25/FY26)

Prepared 2026-10-10 for the brand-markup value-chain research. Every figure is either disclosed (d) in the cited document or computed (c) by me from figures on the cited page(s). n/d = not disclosed or not captured in the extracted text. Page references ("p") are PDF page indices from pdftotext, not printed page numbers. Source IDs (S1-S46) are listed at the end. FY26 documents were used with their FY25 comparatives; FY25 annual reports were not downloaded separately.

Method and limits (short):
- Filing discovery used the ASX Markit Digital announcement API, which returns only the latest five items per query, so a market-sensitive filter was used to surface results releases. PDFs were fetched from ASX announcement and file endpoints, from company IR sites (Wesfarmers, Metcash, Universal Store, James Hardie) and from two third-party mirrors (investorpa.com, bulletin.webull.com), which are flagged where used.
- robots.txt was checked for asx.com.au, metcash.com, wesfarmers.com.au and fphcare.com; no disallowed paths were used. investsmart.com.au returned HTTP 403 for the ARB page and was not retried or bypassed.
- The per-turn WebSearch budget (200 calls, shared across agents) was exhausted before the remaining searches. ARB primary filings, NZ-listed names, CSR and Johns Lyng status, GUD FY26 and Pental could not be resolved.
- Column order was verified from statement headers or narrative for every figure used. FPH (p131), Kogan (p12) and Ansell (p13) present FY25 first; the others read FY26 first.

### Takeaway
FY26 gross margins run from 22% (JB Hi-Fi group, multi-brand electronics retail) to 83% (Lovisa, jewellery). Own-brand and exclusive-heavy retailers and brand owners mostly sit at 40-83%. Multi-brand electronics retail sits at 22-30%. Distributor-layer GM is disclosed only for Bapcor (45%) and Big River (27%); Reece, Metcash, Harvey Norman and Wesfarmers did not disclose group GM in the documents retrieved.

### Inferences
- Own-brand and exclusive-heavy retailers and brand owners earn 40-83% GM (BBN 41%, TPW 32%, SSG 46%, UNI 63%, PMV 65%, NCK 66%, LOV 83%), while multi-brand retail sits at 22-46% (JB Hi-Fi 22%, SUL 46%). The pattern is consistent with the brand layer capturing the margin, but these filings do not prove it.
- Distribution operating margins look thin even where GM is not disclosed: Reece EBIT 5.7% (c), Metcash Hardware & Tools EBIT 4.8% (c). Big River's 26.5% GM is the only disclosed distributor GM at or below 30%.
- Re-sourcing away from China is disclosed by Breville (85% of 120-volt GP dollars sourced outside China), RWC and KMD (factory divestment). None of these disclosures quantifies the cost saving.

### Gaps
- No primary ARB filing was retrieved, so ARB's Thailand manufacturing and its margins are unverified.
- Own-brand or private-label share (% of sales) is not disclosed in extracted text for Kogan, Adairs, Bapcor, Super Retail, Myer, Lovisa and Wesfarmers (the sourcing-country table at p204 was not captured).
- Inventory turns are disclosed only for JB Hi-Fi (6.5x). Turns for the others were not computed because inventory balances were not extracted.
- No document retrieved gives factory-to-shelf or FOB/landed-cost figures, so factory-to-shelf multiples cannot be read directly from these filings.

## 2. Thick and thin layers, direct-import and own-brand disclosures

### Takeaway
Thick layers (GM 50% or more) are dominated by own-brand or exclusive-heavy retailers and by brand owners with specialty or DTC positioning. Thin layers (30% or less) are multi-brand electronics retail (JB Hi-Fi) and one building-products distributor (Big River). The most explicit direct-import or own-brand disclosures are JB Hi-Fi's third-party private-label supplier model, Breville's out-of-China sourcing, Temple & Webster's direct private-label sourcing (51% exclusive revenue) and Baby Bunting's 50.3% exclusive and private-label share. No filing gives a factory-to-shelf multiple.

### Cited Findings
(a) Thickest layers, FY26 GM at or above 50% (d unless marked c): Lovisa 82.6% (S36a p1); Beacon 68.6% statutory (c) (S5 p14); Nick Scali 65.6% (S33 p7); Premier 65.2% (S41b p2); Dusk 64.6% (S18 p1); Universal Store 62.5% (S25 p1); Michael Hill 60.5% (S38 p11); City Chic 59.9% (S43 p14); Adairs 59.0% (S29 p10); KMD 57.7% group (S37a p3); Accent 53.1% (S20 p2). Step One is 61.6% (S27 p19), or 75.9% excluding the obsolescence provision (S27 p2). FPH at 63.7% (S16 p127) is a manufacturer and only a comparator.

(b) Thinnest layers, GM at or below 30%: JB Hi-Fi group 22.4% (c) (S34 p91); JB Hi-Fi Australia 21.94%, The Good Guys 23.74%, e&s 29.72% (S34 p4). ### Inferences
- The thickest layers are where the brand owner or own-brand retailer sets the price. The thinnest are where a multi-brand retailer resells third-party brands. This is an inference from the margin pattern, not a stated finding in any document.
- Own-brand sourcing is being moved out of China at Breville, RWC and KMD. These filings give no margin effect of that move.

### Gaps

- See Section 1 Gaps for own-brand share, sourcing countries and inventory turns.

## 3. Caveats: segment mixing, services revenue, FY differences (d)

### Takeaway
GM and margin definitions differ by company and by period. Cross-company comparisons in Section 1 are indicative only; the (c) and (d) flags show which figures were computed and which were disclosed.

### Cited Findings
- Segment mixing: Wesfarmers segment revenue includes other revenue (S31 p154). Industrial and Safety's FY25 revenue includes Coregas, sold in FY25 (S31 p21, p53); Blackwoods is not split out. Super Retail's "total segment EBIT" of 388.8 (S9 p13) does not equal the sum of the segment EBIT lines (226.0 + 132.4 + 66.4 = 424.8; S9 p16, p18, p20). The basis of the total is not reconciled in the extracted text.
- Metcash revenue measures differ: statutory A$17.35bn versus A$19.63bn including charge-through (S10 p7). The EBIT margin in Section 1 uses the charge-through figure, as the presentation defines it (S11 p30 footnote 4).
- Harvey Norman system sales (A$9.64bn) include franchisee sales and are not HVN revenue. PBT is not comparable to EBIT margins (S12 p1).
- Accent Group's total sales (A$1.64bn) is a non-IFRS measure that includes Athlete's Foot franchise sales (S20 p1 footnote 2). Margins computed on it are not IFRS margins.
- Myer's operating gross profit (A$1,603.2m) is non-IFRS and is not GM (S40 p1).
- Cettire: margins use sales revenue (A$718.4m), not gross revenue (A$953.4m, pre-refund); the "delivered margin" is a company KPI (S45 p1).
- Premier Investments' Appendix 4E includes Breville financial data (revenue 1,810,896 at S41a p65). That is Breville's, not Premier's, and was excluded.
- JB Hi-Fi: e&s gross margin rose partly because of migration from wholesale to agency sales, which are recognised as commission (S34 p4). Revenue basis changed, so the segment GM is not like-for-like.
- Michael Hill: comparable EBIT (A$24.0m, S38 p8) is non-IFRS. The "EBIT to sales" figures of 4.7% and 2.9% (S38 p11) do not have a captured column order and were not used.
- Services and franchise revenue are included in reported revenue for Harvey Norman (franchise fees and property income), Fleetwood (community villages) and JB Hi-Fi e&s (agency commission). Their margins are not separable from product margins.
- Periods: James Hardie and Fisher & Paykel Healthcare report to 31 March 2026. Premier reports 52 weeks to 25 July 2026. Metcash's FY26 results were released 22 June 2026. Most other companies have June year-ends, several on a 52-week basis.
- Currency: James Hardie, RWC and Ansell report in US$. FPH is presumed to report in NZ$ (not confirmed on the extracted page). Others are A$.
- Mirrored sources (flagged): Shaver Shop (S22), Dusk release (S18), Adairs announcement and presentation (S28, S29), Step One (S27), Beacon (S5), Dusk presentation (S19) and Cettire (S45). Their figures match their own narrative text but were not checked against the original ASX files.
- Secondary-only: ARB. Two secondary sources give FY26 revenue of A$702.0m and A$711.5m. Neither was verified, so ARB is excluded from the figures.
- GM definitions differ (for example, the cost of goods sold line may or may not include freight, duty and distribution). These were not normalised.

### Gaps (scope)

- CSR and Johns Lyng: the ASX Markit API returned no announcements for either code. Their status is unverified and no figures were extracted.
