# Real factories behind double hammocks with steel stands (and stands alone): China source map, supplier classification, first-order realism

_Researched 2026-10-10. Evidence grades (user's rubric): **A** = official registry production scope + industrial address + insured headcount, OR US customs B/L showing it as the direct shipper to Western brands, OR certificate naming it as manufacturing site, OR design-patent holder; **B** = own site with ICP + licence + production footage, association membership, trade-show booth as manufacturer, named on a Western brand's label; **C** = platform badges / self-description only. "Factory" only with two independent A/B items. Customs = ImportGenius public static pages (US ocean B/L summaries: last 10 B/Ls, top 5 partners, totals; paid detail not accessed)._

## Q1. Who actually makes the stands (tube bending, welding, powder coating) and the hammock fabric, versus who only trades them?

### Takeaway
US customs records show that most hammock-stand containers bound for Western brands leave Ningbo under **trading-company names**. Ningbo Prime ships for Algoma, Patio Garden Direct, Best Choice (to 2016) and Sunnydaze. Zhejiang Hengming ships for Vivere. Kangshuai and Baoer shipped for Twin Oaks. Costway's importer (Goplus Corp, same Fontana address) buys through the in-group agent Ningbo Joys. A smaller set of **direct shippers with hammock-only product mixes** sits behind them:
- **Ningbo Deja Leisure Products**: Best Choice Products' stands.
- **Ningbo Danlong Furniture**: Lidl US and Techfaith. It also holds US design patent D957174 for a hammock stand.
- **Jarder Home Ningbo**: SUNCREAT and Amazon sellers. Holds design patent D1119418.
- **Longyou Huarun Tourism Products** and **Zhejiang Excellent Outdoor / Longyou Excellent Change**: The Hammock Source, Algoma/Menards, Sunnydaze and Amazon sellers.
- **Yongkang Sunon Leisure**: Twin Oaks.

Only **Danlong** (patent + customs) and **Jarder Home** (patent + customs; office-type address) clear the two-A/B bar. Deja, Longyou Huarun, Zhejiang Excellent and Yongkang Sunon each have one A-type item (customs) and are "probable factories". No registry, insured-headcount or audit document could be reached for any of them, so no tube-bending, welding or powder-coating line was independently confirmed. The only line claim found is Xusheng's self-described "1 new auto powder coating line" (C). No hammock-fabric weaver was identified with A/B evidence.

### Inferences
- The Western brands' **trader-to-factory chain is real and long-lived**. Hengming has supplied Guelph-based hammock importers since at least 2009–2010 (Hammock Village) and Vivere through 2026. Algoma has bought 352 containers through Prime. The trader names on B/Ls hide the source, which surfaces only occasionally (e.g. "DANLONG HAMMOCKS" in a Prime PO).
- The **Ningbo hammock-stand nucleus**: Danlong, Deja, Jarder, Minghuang, Ledu, Hopfung. All hold stand patents and/or show hammock-only customs mixes.
- The **Longyou (Quzhou) hammock nucleus**: Huarun, Excellent Outdoor / Excellent Change. Customs shows these as the direct sources for mid-market US hammock brands. They are the most promising places to look for the actual bending, welding and powder-coating lines.
- Yongkang appears (Sunon), but it is not dominant in recent data.
- Linhai/Taizhou firms dominate wider outdoor furniture (Best Choice, Lowe's, Costco) but not, in what was visible, hammock stands.
- 1688 data points to Bazhou (Hebei) metal-frame and Taihe (Anhui) hammock-textile clusters for the domestic market.
- Hangzhou/Shaoxing textile mills and Anping (Hebei) did not surface in any hammock record seen.

## Q3. Is there an origin design (model numbers, patent) behind the identical "double hammock + 9–10 ft steel stand" SKUs?

### Takeaway
No live patent explains the sameness. The basic "space-saving" steel stand goes back to 1987–2004 US patents that have now lapsed or were fee-expired:
- Branch 1991: "three elongated structural members joined together axially at oblique angles… stabilizing cross pieces".
- Rawlins 1987: collapsible.
- Taiwan Shin Yeh 2004: foldable.
- Hatteras 2004: knock-down, "tubular members of similar dimensions… interchangeable… small box".

So any tube shop can build it. Recent Chinese design patents are ornamental or packaging variants:
- Danlong D957174 (2021–22).
- Ledu D1041220 (2022).
- Minghuang D1047557, US12201209 and CN217243086U — the last has a detachable base explicitly "to reduce carton volume".
- Jarder Home D1119418 (2024).
- Yotrio D1083471 (2023).

The commodity reference SKU is the "Amazon Basics Double Hammock with 9-Foot Space Saving Steel Stand and Carrying Case, 400 lb". A Ningbo trader copies its title and packed data verbatim. Vivere's equivalent codes are "C9…" ("HAMMOCK WITH 9' STAND").

### Inferences
- Gardeon's and Costway's AU sets match the light-gauge class (32 mm tube, ~12 kg). In US data that class is typified by Zhejiang Excellent Outdoor / Excellent Change cartons (11–12 kg) and by Ningbo trader listings. That is a resemblance, not an identification.
- The way to identify the source is to buy one unit and read the carton and manual. Chinese makers often print a factory code, QC sticker or "Ningbo/Longyou" address. Alternatively, ask the factory candidates (Danlong, Deja, Longyou Huarun, Excellent) to match the Gardeon spec sheet.
