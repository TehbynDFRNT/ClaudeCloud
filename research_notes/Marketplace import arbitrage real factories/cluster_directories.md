# Real manufacturers in six Chinese industrial clusters: directory and government-list cross-check

Researched 2026-10-10 (UTC). Scope: trampolines; weights (bumper and cast-iron plates, kettlebells, dial dumbbells); hammock stands and outdoor-leisure steel; electric standing-desk frames and columns; timber cubby houses; aluminium camp kitchens and camp furniture. Aim: give cross-checkers named manufacturers with the source for each, so a supplier on Alibaba or Made-in-China can be tested against a list a reseller would not normally appear on.

Conventions. Names are copied as the source prints them. Every row carries the URL it came from. "Raw-verified" means the name was found in the raw HTML of the cited page. "Fetch summary" means the name came from a WebFetch answer and was not raw-checked. "Search summary" means it came only from a WebSearch snippet summary. "Earlier note" means it came from china_factory_sourcing.md or china_1688_domestic_factories.md in the Australia folder and was not re-verified this round. 1688 platform badges (超级工厂, 实力商家) are 1688's own labels, not a check of factory status. Anything not found is listed under Gaps, not filled in.

Headline. (1) No association member list, 专精特新 list, 高新技术企业 list or 单项冠军 list was reached for any cluster. The only official-type sources reached were two 2025 Xinhua pieces on Ningjin and a 2026 Heze local newspaper piece on Caoxian exhibitors. (2) The Ningjin firm counts conflict across sources (see Q8). (3) Weights has the most named candidates. Trampolines have 1688 cards in Zhejiang and Henan but no Jiangsu or Shandong trampoline maker. Desks have Changzhou 1688 cards. Cubby houses, camp kitchens and hammock stands have almost no verified manufacturer names.
