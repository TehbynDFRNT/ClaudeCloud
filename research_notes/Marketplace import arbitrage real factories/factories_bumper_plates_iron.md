# Who actually makes China's bumper plates, cast-iron plates, kettlebells and 40 kg dial dumbbells (factories vs resellers)

*Research date: 10 Oct 2026. Scope: rubber/urethane Olympic bumper plates first; cast-iron plates, kettlebells and 40 kg dial-adjustable dumbbells second. No supplier was contacted.*

*Access notes:*
- *ImportYeti returned HTTP 403 to curl and WebFetch. ImportYeti facts below come from search-engine summaries of its pages.*
- *ImportGenius public importer/supplier pages were reachable (robots.txt "Allow: /"; only /search is disallowed, and only for named bots). They show the shipper, consignee, product description, gross weight, arrival date and partner tables with container counts. Most product-level facts below are from ImportGenius pages fetched on 10 Oct 2026.*
- *ImportGenius caveats: its "Country of Origin" field often reads "South Korea" for China shipments, probably transhipment via Busan. Product text is as the shipper declared it. Each page shows only about ten recent bills of lading. Coverage is US imports; there is no Australian data.*
- *Google Patents robots.txt allows only /patent/ pages, so patents were read one page at a time and further rights-holders were mined from cited-by tables.*
- *GSXT, Qichacha and Tianyancha were not used (blocked in the earlier round; no CAPTCHA was attempted).*
- *The shared WebSearch quota ran out mid-task, so the rest of the work used direct URLs only.*

*Evidence grades follow the brief:*
- *A = registry production scope with industrial address and insured headcount; a customs record as direct shipper to a Western brand; a certificate naming the manufacturing site; or a patent holding.*
- *B = own site with ICP, licence image and production footage; association membership; a trade-show booth as manufacturer; or being named on a Western brand's label.*
- *C = platform badges or self-description only.*

## Q1. Where is the real production, and which companies demonstrably own the presses, moulds and foundries?

### Takeaway
US customs lines show where Western brands actually buy.

**Rubber bumper plates:**
- Qingdao (Jimo, Chengyang)
- Linyi (Linshu County)
- Rizhao (Wulian County), all in Shandong
- Wangdu (Baoding, Hebei)
- Tong'an (Xiamen, Fujian)

**Cast-iron kettlebells and plates:**
- Rizhao: Wulian County, Gaoxin/Heshan, and the economic development zone (EDZ)
- Chuzhou (Anhui)

**Adjustable dumbbells:**
- Jinzhong (Shanxi)
- Taiwanese-managed suppliers shipping from China

**Nantong exporters** ship mainly steel racks, benches, bars and consolidated mixed accessory loads. In the visible bill-of-lading lines, they are not the rubber-plate shippers behind Rogue, REP, Titan, Bells of Steel, Fringe or Core Health; only about ten recent lines per page are public.

Many storefronts that call themselves "factories" are office-based traders, and some of those (Dx Grandway, Vigor, Modun/Leadman) also appear in customs data under office or residential addresses.

### Inferences
- **Where bumper plates come from.** In these US records, bumper plates come from Shandong (Qingdao, Linyi, Rizhao), Hebei (Wangdu) and Xiamen. Cast-iron kettlebells and plates come overwhelmingly from Rizhao, especially Wulian County, plus Chuzhou. Nantong's role in this trade is steel strength equipment and consolidation. Platform price lists that bundle "Nantong bumper plates" are therefore often Nantong traders or consolidators selling Shandong/Hebei/Fujian plates.
- **Repeat suppliers and identical SKUs.** A handful of producers recur across many brands: Deluxe Xiamen, Heng Li Heng, Qingdao DH, Hiperlion, Zhuoao, Bull King and Winning Rubber. That fits the user's observation of identical SKUs: a few moulders supply many brands and traders. The full count of Chinese plate moulders is still unknown, because the data is US-only and limited to about ten recent bills of lading per page.
- **HK companies in Rogue's records.** Hong Kong companies (Rich York, Sea Star) carry large container volumes for Rogue. These are trading or sourcing intermediaries even for a top brand, so a Western brand's supply chain also includes middlemen. The factories behind them are not visible.

### Gaps
- **Australian brands:** CORTEX/Lifespan, BRIXX, Everfit, Fitness Master, Powertrain and HCE could not be mapped. ImportGenius and ImportYeti cover US (and some other) imports, not Australia.
- **US mapping limits:** BSN Sports could not be resolved on ImportGenius (slug not found). Synergee's plate source beyond Nantong Modern, Nordic and AZJ was not checked.
- **Plant ownership:** no registry production scope, insured headcount or press/foundry footage was obtained for any producer named above. Customs lines prove the exporter of record, not who owns the press.
- **Not reached:** association lists (宁津县健身器材行业协会, Nantong), 专精特新 lists, China Sport Show and Canton Fair exhibitor lists.

## Q2. Candidate factory profiles and evidence grades

### Takeaway
On the brief's rule of two independent A/B items:
- **Bumper-plate producers that qualify:** none yet with two different evidence types. The best bumper-plate candidates hold an A item (customs as direct shipper of "BUMPER PLATE" to several Western brands) but lack a second, independent A/B item. They are Deluxe (Xiamen), Heng Li Heng (Wangdu), Qingdao DH (Jimo), Qingdao Hiperlion, Shandong Zhuoao and Ri Zhao Bull King.

- **Qualify as factories:** Chuzhou Hercules (customs + patents, A + A) and Nantong Ironman (patent + customs + own site). Neither is shown making rubber bumper plates.
- **Tengtai** qualifies for its own steel goods and is a consolidator for plates.

Yes. Customs data shows the real plate producers routinely shipping single ~17–20 t containers to small and mid-size North American brands:
- Qingdao DH → Rage Fitness;
- Winning Rubber → Rage Fitness;
- Heng Li Heng → Carolina Fitness;
- Hiperlion → Titan;
- Zhuoao → Gibson / CPG.

Tengtai shipped ~20.8 t to a small US 3PL buyer, and the trader Dx Grandway ships ~17 t bumper containers to PRX Performance. Published MOQs are 0.1–1 t. List FOB for standard bumpers is about US$1.00–1.50/kg, with no consistent trader premium on platform lists. Expect:
- **payment:** 30% T/T deposit, with the balance before shipment or against a B/L copy;
- **lead time:** 15 days to 3 months;
- **logistics:** exporters with their own import-export rights shipping FOB Qingdao, Xiamen, Nantong, Shanghai or Tianjin.

### Inferences
- **Container size and price tier (inference).**
  - A 150–211 × 100 kg order (15–21 t) matches the 17–20 t single-container orders these producers already ship to small US brands such as Rage Fitness, Carolina Fitness Equipment, Gibson Athletic and PRX.
  - The typical load is about 1,340–1,650 plates per container (Heng Li Heng → Carolina).
  - Nothing suggests these producers require 40HQ or trader-only buying. Plates are weight-limited, so a 40HQ adds no payload under Australian caps.
  - A 15–21 t order sits in the ≥10 t price tier at suppliers that publish tiers.
- **Price band (inference).**
  - About US$1.00–1.30/kg FOB for standard black or colour recycled/virgin-blend bumpers with steel inserts, at ≥10 t.
  - US$1.30–1.70/kg for virgin rubber, stainless hubs, tight tolerance or urethane.
  - List prices from traders (Grandway, Dili, Shangshuo) sit at or below "factory" lists, so the factory-direct saving can only be measured through spec-for-spec RFQs. Send the RFQs to customs-verified moulders: Qingdao DH, Hiperlion, Heng Li Heng, Deluxe Xiamen, Zhuoao and Bull King.
- **Required diligence (inference).**
  - Pay the 30% deposit only to the entity that will appear as shipper on the B/L.
  - Release the balance after an independent pre-shipment inspection of weight tolerance, hub bonding and odour/PAH reports.
  - Ask for press-line video plus a business licence whose 经营范围 includes 制造, to separate moulders from consolidators such as Tengtai or traders such as Grandway.
