# David & Goliath — a nova in two voices

A hyperreal cosmic short film (2 min 36 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps) that retells David and Goliath as a tiny
**white dwarf** and a colossal **red giant**. The giant's overwhelming mass becomes fuel for the smaller star:
matter torn through the inner Lagrange point spirals into a vortex around the dwarf, compresses into a hydrogen
ocean on its surface and detonates as a **classical nova**. The white-hot shell fractures into gold; the small
core endures.

Every frame is rendered in code (WebGL2 fragment shaders in headless Chromium, Canvas 2D for drawings and
titles); the score combines the United States Air Force Band's recording of Vivaldi's *Winter* with an eighties
synth pulse, approaching cannon fire (real cannon from the US Army Band's *1812 Overture*), an electronic pressure
wave and a Doppler roar, mixed in Web Audio.

* **Film:** `dist/david-and-goliath.mp4` (see the final section for checks)
* **Timeline contract:** `film-plan.json` (generated by `tools/build_plan.mjs`)
* **Art bible / engine contract:** `docs/ART-BIBLE.md`

## Structure
| Act | Time | Music (Vivaldi, *L'inverno* I) | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|
| I · Attraction | 0:00–0:34 | bars 1–11: trembling chords stacked voice by voice | the abyss, Goliath, David, the Roche figure-eight in ice-blue and in Leonardo's ink |
| II · Resistance | 0:34–0:58 | bars 12–19: solo violin, *orrido vento* | the stream rips through L1; orbital parabolas; Prometheus's stolen fire |
| II · Compression | 0:58–1:18 | bars 20–26: tutti, *correre e batter li piedi* | the vortex engulfs the dwarf; atoms; Leonardo's deluge answered by the real spiral |
| II · Frenzy | 1:18–1:34 | bars 27–31: 32nd-note runs | 24 beat-cut perspectives: plasma, filaments, sparks, nuclei, drawings, vistas |
| II · Ladder | 1:34–1:54 | bars 32–38: five strikes on the downbeats + tremolo | five cannon-strikes, each a closer, more compressed view |
| III · Ignition | 1:54–2:05 | **music cut** → electronic pressure wave, descending Doppler roar | white-out; the frame opens from 2.39:1 to 16:9; eruption; the shock front passes |
| IV · Aftermath | 2:05–2:36 | bars 56–63: closing tutti + fermata (eight bars) | expansion, devastation, survival; the rings become ink |

## How it was made
* **Music analysis** (`tools/analyze_music.py`, `tools/refine_grid.py`): the Mutopia engraving of RV 297 (used only as
  an analysis reference, not redistributed) is aligned to the recording with chroma DTW; a dynamic-programming fit
  places every bar line where onset evidence at all eight eighth-notes is strongest under a smooth-tempo prior.
  Result: `analysis/grid.json` (bar/eighth times, bass line). The edit points are signal-derived, not verified by
  listening: ignition = end of the bar-37/38 tremolo (source 113.54 s); aftermath = bars 56–63 from the bar-56
  dominant downbeat (source 166.65 s); strikes = tutti hits on the downbeats of bars 33–37 (96.54–108.76 s).
* **Timeline**: `tools/build_plan.mjs` writes the 61-shot plan from musical positions; picture, cannon cues,
  flashes, letterbox and the synth score all derive from it. `tools/validate_plan.py` checks coverage and sync.
* **Picture**: `src/engine` is a frame-addressable WebGL2 HDR pipeline (scene → bloom pyramid → anamorphic
  streak → ACES → grade → grain → letterbox). `src/scenes/*` are the departments (red giant, white dwarf,
  binary system, accretion vortex, plasma, atoms, Renaissance studies, nova). Physics in
  `src/scenes/lib/binary.js` (Roche potential, L1, ballistic stream integration) drives both the 3D matter and the
  ink drawings.
* **Sound**: `src/audio/score.js` renders the full mix in an `OfflineAudioContext` (`tools/render-audio.mjs`).

## Reproduce
```bash
npm install                                   # playwright-core (uses the system Chromium at /opt/pw-browsers)
pip install librosa pretty_midi               # only to re-run the music analysis
node tools/build_plan.mjs && python3 tools/validate_plan.py film-plan.json
node tools/render-audio.mjs                   # -> out/audio/mix.wav
node tools/render.mjs film --w 1920           # -> out/frames/00000.jpg ... (resumable)
node tools/encode.mjs                         # -> dist/david-and-goliath.mp4 + verification report
```
Useful while developing: `node tools/render.mjs sandbox <scene> --preset <shotId> --times 0,2,4`,
`node tools/render.mjs shot <shotId> --n 3`, `node tools/render.mjs sheet --shots`.
