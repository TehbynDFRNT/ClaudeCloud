# A nova in three voices — David & Goliath · Sol Invictus · Prometheus

A hyperreal cosmic short film, told three ways. Each cut runs 2 min 46 s at 1080×1920 (9:16), 24 fps.

A tiny **white dwarf** feeds on a colossal **red giant**:
- Matter torn through the inner Lagrange point spirals into a vortex around the dwarf.
- The vortex compresses into a hydrogen ocean on the dwarf's surface.
- At ignition the picture goes **dark**: the climax is heard, not seen. Then the **nova** erupts from the darkness.
- The shell fractures into gold and the small core endures.
- A last shot shows the same event from a dark-sky Earth: under the Milky Way, *a new star* appears.

The three cuts share one cosmic picture and one score. They differ in what the story calls its two stars, in two
of the frenzy drawings, and in the marble figure that turns its gaze toward us across the whole film.

| Cut | Plan | Giant / dwarf | Marble figure | Frenzy studies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **David & Goliath** | `film-plan.json` | GOLIATH / DAVID | Michelangelo's *David* (head) | a sling |
| **Sol Invictus** | `film-plan-sol.json` | HIEMS (Winter) / SOL INVICTUS | *Alexander as Helios*, its lost sun-rays restored as light | a Sol coin; the winter solstice |
| **Prometheus** | `film-plan-prometheus.json` | ZEUS / PROMETHEUS | a Giant of the Pergamon Altar (rebel against Zeus), standing in for Prometheus | an eagle; chains |

* **Films:** `dist/david-916.mp4`, `dist/sol-916.mp4`, `dist/prometheus-916.mp4`.
* **Timeline contracts:** `film-plan*.json`, generated by `tools/build_plan.mjs`.
* **Briefs:** `docs/ART-BIBLE.md` covers the look and the engine contract. `docs/PORTRAIT.md` covers the 9:16 round.

## Structure
| Act | Time | Music (Vivaldi, *L'inverno* I) | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|
| I · Attraction | 0:00–0:34 | bars 1–11: trembling chords stacked voice by voice | the abyss, the giant, the dwarf, the Roche figure-eight in ice-blue and in Leonardo's ink |
| II · Resistance | 0:34–0:58 | bars 12–19: solo violin, *orrido vento* | the stream rips through L1; orbital parabolas; the stolen fire |
| II · Compression | 0:58–1:18 | bars 20–26: tutti, *correre e batter li piedi* | the vortex engulfs the dwarf; atoms; Leonardo's deluge answered by the real spiral |
| II · Frenzy | 1:18–1:34 | bars 27–31: 32nd-note runs | beat-cut perspectives: plasma, filaments, sparks, nuclei, drawings, the marble gaze |
| II · Ladder | 1:34–1:54 | bars 32–38: five strikes on the downbeats + tremolo | five strikes, each darkening the picture; strike 5 implodes into a point |
| III · Ignition | 1:54–2:05 | **music cut** → the loudest moment: detonation, pressure wave, descending Doppler roar | 2.25 s of darkness; the eruption emerges from it; the 3:4 window has opened to 9:16 unseen |
| IV · Aftermath | 2:05–2:32 | bars 56–63: closing tutti + fermata | expansion, devastation, survival; the rings become ink; the figure stares straight at us under the title |
| Coda | 2:32–2:46 | night air, a silent shimmer | the Milky Way over a dark horizon; a new star appears. *The birth of a new star; the Nova.* |

**The marble figure.** There are sixteen inserts (M01–M16), most about a second long. Each shows a single statue on black and is a different cinematic angle on it.
- Across the whole film the figure turns exactly 90°. It is in profile at its first appearance (0:21) and looks straight into the lens at the end (M16).
- An insert cuts into the shot it lands on. That shot keeps its own clock (`span`), so the approved motion is never sped up.

## How it was made
* **Music analysis** (`tools/analyze_music.py`, `tools/refine_grid.py`).
  - The Mutopia engraving of RV 297 is aligned to the recording with chroma DTW. It is used only as an analysis reference and is not redistributed.
  - A dynamic-programming fit then places every bar line where onset evidence at all eight eighth-notes is strongest, under a smooth-tempo prior. The result is `analysis/grid.json`.
* **Timeline.** `tools/build_plan.mjs` writes the three plans from musical positions. Picture, cannon cues, effects, letterbox, text and the synth score all derive from them. `tools/validate_plan.py` checks coverage and sync.
* **Picture.**
  - `src/engine` is a frame-addressable WebGL2 HDR pipeline: scene → bloom pyramid → anamorphic streak → ACES → grade → grain → letterbox → titles.
  - Every scene can carry `portrait` overrides. A camera reframing hook (roll, zoom, pan, dolly) recomposes the approved 16:9 photography for 9:16 without changing it.
  - `src/scenes/*` are the departments: red giant, white dwarf, binary system, accretion vortex, plasma, atoms, Renaissance studies, nova, the dark (`void`), the dark-sky Earth (`earthsky`) and the marble figure (`statue`).
  - The statue rasterises three public-domain 3D scans (`media/scenes/statue/`, prepared by `tools/prepare_statues.py`) with marble shading and a per-figure aura.
* **Sound.** `src/audio/score.js` renders the full mix in an `OfflineAudioContext` (`tools/render-audio.mjs`). The mix is −16 LUFS integrated, with the climax around −6.7 LUFS short-term and a true peak of −1 dBTP.
* **Distributed render.** `tools/chunk.mjs` renders 10 s blocks made of per-shot pieces.
  - Each piece is encoded from its own keyframe and has its own content fingerprint, so a changed shot re-renders only its own frames.
  - Pieces identical between cuts are rendered once.
  - Helper sessions render ranges in parallel, and assembly stream-copies the pieces and adds the soundtrack.

## Reproduce
```bash
npm install                                   # playwright-core (uses the system Chromium at /opt/pw-browsers)
node tools/build_plan.mjs                     # -> film-plan.json, film-plan-sol.json, film-plan-prometheus.json
node tools/render-audio.mjs                   # -> out/audio/mix.wav (166.083 s, shared by the three cuts)
for p in film-plan.json film-plan-sol.json film-plan-prometheus.json; do
  node tools/chunk.mjs render --plan $p        # -> dist/<cut>/ pieces (resumable; ranges with --from/--to)
  node tools/chunk.mjs assemble --plan $p      # -> dist/<cut>.mp4 + verification report
done
```
Useful while developing:
- `node tools/render.mjs shot <shotId> --n 3 --w 540`
- `node tools/render.mjs sheet --shots --w 270 --cols 16`
- Add `--plan film-plan-sol.json` (or the Prometheus plan) for another cut.
